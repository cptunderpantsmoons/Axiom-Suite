03 / The handoff

A clean contract at every boundary.

ContextLock exports curated GroundContext. Axiom Compile turns it into a signed task packet. Helm validates, audits, and strips the envelope before forwarding upstream.

  • Deterministic inputs and reproducible outputs
  • Fail-closed schemas across every seam
  • Sanitized audit metadata, never raw prompt bodies
